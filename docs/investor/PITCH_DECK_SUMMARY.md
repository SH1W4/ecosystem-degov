## 🚀 Delivery Tips

1. **Keep it to 10 minutes** (1 min per slide)
2. **Lead with the problem** - Make them feel the pain
3. **Show, don't tell** - Use the Etherscan link as proof
4. **End with urgency** - "We're closing the round in X weeks"
5. **Have a demo ready** - Show the Trinity AI agent in action
